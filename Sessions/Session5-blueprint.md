Five rules to conduct this like a pro

1. Never reveal the answer too early.

Ask students what they think first. Let them defend their decision.

2. Move around the room.

Don't stay in front of the screen. Observe what teams are doing.

3. Ask better questions.

A thoughtful explanation is worth more than a visually attractive screen.

Use short explanations, clear instructions, visible countdowns and quick transitions.
