KITONLY THESE

The kit. Only these six.

If you invent a seventh pattern, ask which of these it actually is.

  1. 01Button. The action. One primary. Everything else is quieter.
  2. 02Input + label + helper. A field the person can fill without guessing.
  3. 03List / card. Choose one of several things.
  4. 04Navigation. Where am I. How do I get back.
  5. 05Empty state. First use. Nothing there yet. What now?
  6. 06Error / success. What the system did. What to do next.

LAWSFIVE

Five laws. For this screen.

Each one is a sentence you can point at on the frame.

  1. 01Fitts. Time to hit a target grows when it is small or far. Make the primary big and close.
  2. 02Hick. Time to choose grows with equal options. One primary. Hide or delay the rest.
  3. 03Jakob. People spend more time on other screens than yours. Use a pattern they already know.
  4. 04Miller. Working memory is small. Do not dump twelve fields on one step.
  5. 05Postel. Be generous on input, strict on what you store. Do not reject a real phone number for a space.
