Your project · 4 hours

Make it. Then play it.

You make it. You see a weak screen, then a better one. Then we name the UI and the law together. No kit. No AI. Rectangles and type.

The job

Open a screen you had to finish. Stay on it. Do not scroll.

  1. What is this screen? Not the brand. The thing this page is.
  2. Why does it exist? If it was deleted tonight, what job fails tomorrow?
  3. Tell your neighbour in 60 seconds. No design words.
  4. Point at the tap that finishes. What does the screen still need from them?
  5. Wall: mess lunch. A weak reading names the brand. A sharp one names the job, the tap, and what it still needs.
  6. Same two questions for your product. Then write:

A [person] can [task] without [barrier].

If you cannot write that, do not draw yet. Then open figma.com. Design file, not FigJam. Education plan if you have a student email.

One file per group. Two people at the keyboard, max.

1 · Constrain

Press F. Phone / 390 × 844. Name the frame with the sentence.

Loose rectangles on an infinite canvas are not a product. The phone is the brief.

2 · Type

Press T. Three lines only: the job, one fact, what happens next. Then change the size on the right so the job is biggest.

UI: hierarchy. Law: Miller — do not load the mind with equal lines. Big type is the job. Small type is the helper.

3 · Commit

Press R. One rectangle at the bottom. Wide. Type the verb from your sentence on it.

UI: the button is the commit. Law: Fitts — bigger, closer targets are faster. The verb must be a thumb target, not a speck.

4 · Ask

  1. What does the system not know yet? One field. Label above. Helper below. Delete every field this step does not need.
  2. Must they pick, or can they skip? If they must pick, draw two or three. Mark a default. If they can skip, write that on the frame.

UI: input = label + field + helper. A list is a choice. Laws: Jakob — forms should work like forms they know. Miller — ask only this step. Hick — equal options freeze the hand. Duplicate a row with ⌘D.

5 · States

Select the phone frame. ⌘D twice. One copy: nothing is there yet. One copy: they already tapped. Change only what happened. Add a title and a way out on every frame.

UI: empty and after are still screens with a verb. Navigation is title + a way out. Laws: feedback — say what the system did. Postel — accept a real number; be strict in what you store.

6 · Play

Top right: Prototype. Click the verb. Drag to the after frame. Press play. One noodle. No Smart Animate.

The simple pay UX: Pay. Then paid. Your file does the same — the verb must land.

UI: a prototype is a tap that goes somewhere. Law: feedback — they feel what the system did. If play does nothing, it is still a poster.

7 · Craft

Same phone. Three checks. No new frame.

  1. Squint. If the job vanishes, raise contrast.
  2. Pull the label onto the field. That is proximity (Gestalt).
  3. Make one thing look tappable. That is a signifier. Affordance is what it can do. The signifier is how they know. Von Restorff: the odd one is remembered — one lime, the rest quiet.

Tesler: do not dump the system's work on them. Extra fields and extra equals are your complexity, sitting on their thumb.

Swap

  1. Share → anyone with the link can view.
  2. They tap your verb. You stay quiet.
  3. Log: done / stuck / where.
  4. Change three things. Sticky: the change, and a name from the wall (UI fundamental or law).

Fitts — cannot hit it. Hick — too many equals. Jakob — never seen that control. Miller — too many fields. Postel — a real number failed. Tesler — they are doing the system's work. Von Restorff — nothing stands out. Feedback — silence after tap. Contrast — vanished when you squint. Proximity — label not on the field. Signifier — you had to say “tap here.”

Done when a stranger can tap the verb and see what happened. Paste the link. Check in a private window.

Keys

V select · F frame · R rectangle · T text · ⌘D duplicate · Prototype tab · play · ⌘0 zoom · ⌘Z undo

Do not open Auto Layout, kits, or Figma AI. Ask the person next to you.
