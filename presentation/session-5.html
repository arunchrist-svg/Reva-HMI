A · Getting started

Downloaded for the first time. Too many fields create friction. She needs the next step to feel safe. Voice: “I just want to try meditation. Why so many questions?” HMW: Help Arya get started without feeling overwhelmed? Build: Welcome, registration, mobile, OTP, success. Validation, privacy, errors, progress.

B · Personalisation

Wants relevance, does not know the jargon. Everyday language. Too many questions feel like an interrogation. Preferences must be editable later. Voice: “I don't know what kind of meditation I need. I just want to feel less stressed.” HMW: Understand Arya's needs without a lengthy questionnaire? Build: Intro, goals, experience, duration, reminders, completion. Radio, checkbox, chips, switch, optional.

C · Home

Opens before work. Five minutes. She wants to continue quickly. Too many options delay the decision. Progress should encourage. Voice: “Just tell me what I can do today.” HMW: Make Arya's next action immediately understandable? Build: Dashboard, daily recommendation, continue, inspiration, quick actions. Hierarchy, cards, empty.

D · Discover

Wants help sleeping. Does not know the search term. She may search by mood. Duration matters. Browsing still needs guidance. Voice: “I don't know what to search for. I just want something to help me sleep.” HMW: Help Arya find content even when she doesn't know the name? Build: Explore, categories, search, results, filters, details. Chips, IA, empty results.

E · Meditation

Ten-minute guided session selected. Minimal distraction. Reliable controls. Interruptions happen. Completion should be obvious. Voice: “I just want to press play, close my eyes and listen.” HMW: Make the meditation experience effortless and dependable? Build: Details, intro, player, pause, resume, completion, feedback.

F · Goals

Meditated four days. Missed yesterday. Missed days hurt motivation. Unrealistic goals shame her. Progress should encourage, not scold. Voice: “I missed one day. Does that mean I've failed?” HMW: Help Arya build a habit without making her feel guilty? Build: Goal setup, daily goal, progress, challenges, achievements, missed-day recovery.

G · Journal

Session done. Wants to reflect. She may not have words. Mood chips may be enough. Reflections feel private. Voice: “Sometimes I don't know what I'm feeling. I just want a simple way to capture it.” HMW: Help Arya reflect without making journaling another task? Build: Landing, mood, prompts, writing, save, past entries. Privacy, skip, empty.

H · Profile

Wants to change reminder time. Settings are hard to find. Controls must be predictable. Account actions need clarity. Voice: “I changed something when I joined. I can't remember where.” HMW: Give Arya confidence and control over her experience? Build: Profile, details, preferences, notifications, account. Switches, lists, confirmations.

PERSONAONE USER

Arya, 30.

Working professional. New to meditation. Busy. Usually 5–15 minutes. Wants less stress, better sleep, a habit that survives a week.

Arya will

  • Open the app in a corridor.
  • Give you minutes, not an hour.
  • Quit if the first screen lectures.

Arya will not

  • Learn specialist vocabulary to start.
  • Fill a novel before Arya tries.
  • Forgive streak shame for one missed day.
