NAMEBEST PRACTICE

What a strong prompt includes.

For research questions on your idea — write these into the ask.

  • 01Role — who the AI should act as (e.g. UX researcher).
  • 02Context — your idea in one clear line.
  • 03People — who you will actually talk to.
  • 04Decision — what the answers must help you decide.
  • 05Constraints — non-leading; real past behaviour; no feature wish-list.

Format

01 BRIEFSCOPE

Prompt for the scope.

Draw the line. In, out, and what “done this term” means.

RoleAct as an experienced product designer scoping a student studio project.
ContextProblem, people, and goals: [paste]. Time: this academic term. Team: 5 students.
TaskSet a realistic scope. Protect the team from building everything.
Format
  • 1In — 3–5 things we will research and design.
  • 2Out — 3 things we will not do (and why).
  • 3Constraints — time, place, access, tools.
  • 4Done this term — the smallest shippable: research + prototype + test, not a full company product.

Constraints: Must fit 5 people this term. No payments platform, no hospital-wide IT, no CNC factory. Be ruthless.

02 RESEARCHPLAN

Prompt for the research plan.

Aim, people, method, use. Doable from this room.

RoleAct as an experienced UX researcher writing a one-week field plan.
ContextBrief so far: [paste problem, people, goals, scope].
TaskWrite a research plan we can run. Interviews first. No survey as the main method.
Format
  • 1Aim — one sentence. The decision this study must help.
  • 2People — who, how many (3–6), how we recruit this week.
  • 3Method — 1:1 interviews; where; 30 minutes; what we observe if we can.
  • 4Questions the study must answer — 3, not 15.
  • 5Use — how findings change the brief / design.
  • 6Ethics — consent, recording, what we will not ask.

Constraints: Realistic this week. Named recruit places. No “n=100”. No leading research questions.

02 RESEARCHGUIDE

Prompt for the discussion guide.

A 30-minute 1:1 you would actually take into the room.

RoleAct as an experienced UX researcher writing a non-leading interview guide.
ContextResearch plan: [paste]. People: [paste who we will interview].
TaskWrite a discussion guide for a 30-minute 1:1. Past behaviour only.
Format
  • 1Open — 2 warm questions (3 min). Easy, specific, recent.
  • 2Core — 6 questions. Each has 2 probes (“Tell me about the last time…” / “What did you do next?”).
  • 3Close — 2 questions (what we missed; anything else).
  • 4Timebox — minutes next to each block.

Constraints: Non-leading. Neutral. No “would you use”. No “do you like”. No feature wish-list. Suitable for people we can actually meet.

02 RESEARCHINTERVIEWS

Prompt for how we run interviews.

Recruit, consent, roles, what to capture. The session itself.

RoleAct as an experienced UX researcher coaching a student team in the field.
ContextGuide: [paste]. People: [paste]. We are 5 students. 30-minute interviews.
TaskWrite the interview operating sheet — how we run the session, not more questions.
Format
  • 1Recruit script — 4 sentences we can send or say.
  • 2Consent — what we say before recording or notes.
  • 3Roles — who talks, who notes, who keeps time.
  • 4Minute-by-minute flow — 0–30.
  • 5Capture — quotes, last-time stories, workarounds, exact words for objects/places.
  • 6Stop — what we do if they go generic or start designing features.

Constraints: Two people in the room with the participant, max. Stay quiet after a question. No pitching our idea.
