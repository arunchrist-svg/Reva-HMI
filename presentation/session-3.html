DISSECTBAD PROMPT

Campus Cart · why this ask fails

A bad prompt for this scenario.

  • 01Vague — “campus cart app” with no problem or people.
  • 02No who — who will you interview?
  • 03No decision — what should the answers help you choose?
  • 04No rules — invites leading, opinion, and feature lists.
Prompt Bad 
give me 3 research questions for campus cart app
NAMEBEST PRACTICE

What a strong prompt includes.

For research questions on your idea — write these into the ask.

  • 01Role — who the AI should act as (e.g. UX researcher).
  • 02Context — your idea in one clear line.
  • 03People — who you will actually talk to.
  • 04Decision — what the answers must help you decide.
  • 05Constraints — non-leading; real past behaviour; no feature wish-list.

RoleAct as an experienced UX strategist.
Context[paste your group’s chosen idea or real-world brief].
TaskWrite a sharp project brief that can guide the full project — research through build, deploy, and test.
Format
Fill every section with concrete detail:
  • 1Problem — who is hurt, what is hard today, where it happens, why it matters (one tight paragraph).
  • 2People — 2–3 interviewable user types; for each: role, situation, what to learn.
  • 3Goals — 3 measurable user outcomes + 1 decision this research must unlock.
  • 4Scope — In: 3–5 items. Out: 3 items. Constraints: time, campus/city, access.
  • 5Roadmap — for each phase write What we do + Deliverable + Done when: Brief → Research → Define → Design → Build → Deploy → Test.

Constraints: Specific names and places. Researchable this term. No buzzwords. No feature wish-list. No vague “improve UX” lines.

PROJECTROADMAP

Full project roadmap.

End-to-end UX process — from brief to build, deploy, and test.

  1. 01Brief — problem, people, goals, scope
  2. 02Research — plan, discussion guide, interviews, insights
  3. 03Define — HMW, opportunities, success metrics
  4. 04Design — flows, wireframes, prototype
  5. 05Build — working product in scope
  6. 06Deploy — live for real users
  7. 07Test — usability, learn, iterate
