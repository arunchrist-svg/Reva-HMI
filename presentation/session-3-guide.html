Main task — groups of 5–6

Stay with your idea. Project plan. Research plan. Discussion guide. Publish one public page.

Starter
Act as an experienced UX researcher.
Context: [your project idea, in one paragraph].
Task: Draft a project plan, a research plan, and a discussion guide.
Format:
- Project plan: problem, users, goals, scope, out of scope
- Research plan: aim, people, method, use
- Discussion guide: open, core questions with probes, close
Constraints: Non-leading questions. Realistic for this week. No feature list. Suitable for real interviews.
