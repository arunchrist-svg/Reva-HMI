1. Google AI Plus · most important · ~20 min

This gives you higher limits on Gemini — the tool we use for prompting and research. In India the free student plan is called Google AI Plus (1 year at no cost).

Two common mistakes Use your personal Gmail (@gmail.com), not your college email, to sign in to Gemini. College emails often fail at claim time. Keep a payment method ready — a small ₹2 bank check may appear and is refunded; the 12‑month plan itself is free.

01

Open the student offer page

On your phone or laptop, go to gemini.google/students. Or search Google for Google AI student offer.

02

Sign in with personal Gmail

Top right / sign-in: use your personal @gmail.com account. Do not start with your college ID email for this login.

03

Click “Claim a student plan at no cost”

Same as “Get student offer” in some walkthroughs. You will be sent to SheerID — Google’s student verification partner.

04

Complete SheerID verification

Fill first name, last name, date of birth, and school. Start typing REVA University and pick it from the list.

  • If you have a college email ending in .ac.in, enter it when asked — SheerID may send a confirm link (often the fastest path).
  • If not, choose document upload: clear photo of your college ID or latest fee receipt. Name, photo (if any), college, and current year must be readable.
05

Confirm the free 1‑year plan

After approval, return to Google One / Gemini subscription. You should see ₹0 for 12 months, then ₹399/month after that unless you cancel.

Add a card or UPI AutoPay when asked. This verifies the account; it does not charge the monthly fee now.

Done when Open gemini.google.com signed in. Your plan shows Google AI Plus / student benefits (higher limits, study tools). Screenshot this for yourself.

2. GitHub Student Pack + Copilot · start tonight · approval may wait

GitHub’s student pack unlocks free developer tools. After approval, activate Copilot Student for vibe coding in later sessions.

Watch first (GitHub Student Pack)

Follow along. Skip any “spoof location” tips — apply from campus if you can, or upload a clear ID and wait for review.

Open GitHub Student Pack Alt link (students) Open video on YouTube
01

Create or sign in to GitHub

Go to github.com/signup if you do not have an account. Use an email you check daily. Verify that email.

02

Fill billing / address details first

GitHub → profile photo (top right) → Settings → Billing and plans → payment / billing information. Complete name and address. Students often use their hostel / campus address. Incomplete address is a common rejection reason.

03

Start the student application

Open education.github.com/pack → Sign up for Student Developer Pack (or “Get student benefits”). Choose Student.

04

Select your school

Search for REVA University and select it. If GitHub asks for a school email and you have one (@….ac.in), add it under Settings → Emails, verify it, then continue the application.

05

Share location (when asked)

Allow location if you are on or near campus. If you are off campus and get stuck, upload strong proof (ID + fee receipt) and submit — do not use VPNs for this step.

06

Upload proof and submit

Best option: photo of your college ID. Hold it so all text is sharp (no glare). Fee receipt also works. Submit the application.

Approval: often a few hours, sometimes 1–2 days (occasionally longer). Check your GitHub email and the Education benefits page.

07

After approval: activate Copilot Student

When approved, open your GitHub Education benefits page and activate Copilot / Copilot Student. Confirm in Settings → Copilot that the student plan is on.

Done when (minimum for tomorrow) Application submitted (or already approved). Ideal: Copilot Student activated. If still pending, bring your application confirmation to class — we can work around short delays.

Stuck?

  • Gemini claim fails on college email — switch to personal Gmail and retry the student link.
  • SheerID rejects the photo — retake in bright light; crop so only the ID is visible; try fee receipt instead.
  • Worried about payment — the student year is ₹0. After 12 months it renews at the listed monthly price unless you cancel in Google One.
  • GitHub still pending tomorrow — that is OK if you submitted tonight. Bring the application status page.
  • Need help in class — sit with a neighbour who finished and follow their screen for the stuck step only.
Priority order if short on time: finish Google AI Plus first. Start GitHub second and let approval run overnight.
